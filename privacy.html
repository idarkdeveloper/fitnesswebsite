PulseFit privacy

PulseFit is a personal fitness app. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sells or shares your data.

Health Connect (Android app)

With your permission, the PulseFit Android app reads these from Health Connect, where Samsung Health and your watch store them:

  • Steps, to fill in your daily step count
  • Sleep sessions, to fill in how long you slept
  • Weight, to fill in your weight log
  • Exercise sessions and active calories, to show what your watch recorded on the day

PulseFit never writes to Health Connect. Values are stored in your PulseFit log on the phone. You can turn access off at any time in Health Connect, or under Profile in PulseFit.

Where your data lives

  • By default everything stays on your device.
  • If you sign in to sync, a copy of your log (including the steps, sleep and weight above) is stored in a private database that only your account can read or write, so your devices stay in sync. Delete cloud data in Profile removes it.
