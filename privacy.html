Mizan privacy

Mizan is a personal fitness app. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sells or shares your data.

Health Connect (Android app)

With your permission, the Mizan Android app reads these from Health Connect, where Samsung Health and your watch store them:

  • Steps, to fill in your daily step count
  • Sleep sessions, to fill in how long you slept
  • Weight, to fill in your weight log
  • Body fat, to fill in the body fat % your smart scale's app recorded
  • Exercise sessions and active calories, to show what your watch recorded on the day

Mizan never writes to Health Connect. Values are stored in your Mizan log on the phone. You can turn access off at any time in Health Connect, or under Profile in Mizan.

Shared report images (Android app)

You can share a report image from your smart scale's app to Mizan. The app reads the text in that image on the phone itself to find your weight, body fat, muscle mass and visceral fat, shows you the numbers to check, and saves only the ones you confirm. The image is not stored and is never sent anywhere.

Room thermometer (Android app)

If you set up a SwitchBot meter, the app listens for a few seconds to what the meter broadcasts over Bluetooth to read your room's temperature and humidity. It never connects to the meter or to other devices, does not use Bluetooth to find your location, and keeps the reading on your phone.

Weather

If you have set a place for prayer times, Mizan asks the free weather service Open-Meteo for the forecast there, to tell you whether to walk outside or indoors. Only the place's coordinates, rounded to about a kilometre, are sent; no account or other data.

App updates (Android app)

A few times a day the Android app asks GitHub, where the app's files are published, for the number of the newest version. No personal data is sent with that request. If you tap Update, the app downloads the new version from the same place and opens Android's installer; nothing is installed without your confirmation.

Notifications (Android app)

If you allow notifications, the app schedules your reminders (training days, the weekly meal plan and water) on the phone itself. If you sign in, your reminder settings (times and on/off) are backed up with the rest of your data so they come back on a new phone; the notifications themselves are never sent from a server. Turn them off under Profile, or in Android Settings → Apps → Mizan → Notifications.

Barcode scanning

When you scan a barcode under Add food, the camera is used only while the scan screen is open, and the picture never leaves the phone: only the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts, a free, open food database, to look up the product's nutrition values. You can also type the number instead of using the camera.

Where your data lives

  • By default everything stays on your device.
  • If you sign in to sync, a copy of your log (including the steps, sleep, weight and body fat above) is stored in a private database that only your account can read or write, so your devices stay in sync. Delete cloud data in Profile removes it.
